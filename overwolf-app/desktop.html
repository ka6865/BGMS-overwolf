Two steps to get started

  1. Turn on session handoff below and enter your PUBG nickname.
  2. Play a match. When it ends, the summary travels to BGMS on its own.

Nothing is sent until you turn it on. The overlay works either way, and you can open it in a match with the hotkey listed under overlay settings.
